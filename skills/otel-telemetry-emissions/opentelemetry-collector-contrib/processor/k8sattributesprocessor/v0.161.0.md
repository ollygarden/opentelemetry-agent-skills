The Kubernetes resource attributes this processor adds to telemetry are not inventoried here. Feature gates `processor.k8sattributes.EmitV1K8sConventions` and `processor.k8sattributes.DontEmitV0K8sConventions` are beta (enabled by default) from v0.161.0: stable semconv names such as `k8s.pod.label.<key>`, `k8s.pod.annotation.<key>`, and `container.image.tags` are emitted, and the legacy `k8s.pod.labels.<key>`, `k8s.pod.annotations.<key>`, and `container.image.tag` are not unless the gates are disabled.
